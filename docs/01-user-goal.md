# User Goal

A safety engineer at a local transportation agency needs to produce a
plan for reducing crashes in the area they are responsible for, backed
by data. The plan picks out specific locations where safety
improvements are worth considering, pairs each location with one or
more countermeasures (a countermeasure is a physical fix such as a
signal upgrade or a turn lane), and estimates how many crashes each
fix would prevent.

The plan has to be based on the real crash history of the area, hold
up when the area is compared against similar counties or cities, and
use countermeasures whose effectiveness is already known and
published. The result feeds into safety planning documents, or
supports a grant application.
